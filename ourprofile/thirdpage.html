Ipoh White Coffee

Description: Ipoh White Coffee is a popular coffee drink which originated in Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia. The coffee beans are lightly roasted with palm oil margarine, resulting in a smooth, sweet, and rich flavor. It is usually served with condensed milk.

Location Recommendation: To experience authentic white coffee, you can visit some of the best traditional coffee shops in Ipoh such as:

  • Nam Heong White Coffee
  • Sin Yoon Loong
  • Chang Jiang White Coffee
